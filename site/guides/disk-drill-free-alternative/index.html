A Free Alternative to Disk Drill, With No Recovery Limit

Disk Drill is a polished app, but its free version only goes so far. If you’ve hit that wall, here’s what RecovMe does differently, and where Disk Drill is still the better choice.

By Dhan Denvert AsuncionUpdated 30 October 2026Mac and Windows

Short answer

Disk Drill’s free version recovers up to 100 MB on Windows, and on a Mac it scans and previews but only recovers files that its Recovery Vault was already protecting. RecovMe is a free, open-source alternative for Apple silicon Macs and Windows with no recovery limit. Disk Drill still has features RecovMe doesn’t, listed below.

Written by Dhan Denvert Asuncion, who makes RecovMe. Disk Drill’s limits and prices were checked on CleverFiles’ free-version page (updated 22 March 2026) and its Pro page on 30 October 2026. CleverFiles’ English page has also shown a “Limited Time” 500 MB offer for Windows.

When Disk Drill is the better choice

  • You have an Intel Mac: RecovMe needs Apple silicon.
  • You want Recovery Vault, which keeps track of files before you delete them, or drive health monitoring.
  • You want a company’s paid support behind the app.

When RecovMe is enough

For deleted or formatted photos, videos and documents on SD cards, USB sticks and external drives, RecovMe runs PhotoRec, the same open-source engine whose official site recommends Disk Drill to people who want a graphical interface, plus The Sleuth Kit to bring back original names. Then it sorts everything and lets you save it all, however much there is.

Questions

Is RecovMe related to Disk Drill?

No. RecovMe is an independent open-source app. It isn’t affiliated with CleverFiles, the maker of Disk Drill.

Can RecovMe recover what Disk Drill found in its free scan?

Usually, yes: both search the same drive, and RecovMe saves everything it finds. Stop using the drive in the meantime, so nothing new overwrites the files.

Can either app recover a Mac’s internal drive?

Not files deleted from an Apple silicon Mac’s own drive: the drive is encrypted and deleted files are wiped by the Mac itself. Use the Trash, Time Machine or iCloud for those.

Is RecovMe really free?

Yes, 100%. There’s no trial, no size limit and nothing to unlock after the scan. It’s open source, and donations are optional.
