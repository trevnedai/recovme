RecovMe in short

One line: RecovMe is free, open-source file and photo recovery for Mac and Windows, with no paywall after the scan.

One paragraph: RecovMe brings back deleted and formatted photos, videos, music, recordings, CCTV and dashcam footage and documents from SD cards, USB sticks and hard drives, and copies what survives on Android phones and in iPhone or iPad backups. Unlike most “free” recovery apps, it doesn’t ask you to pay before you can save what it finds: there’s no trial, no size limit and no account. It runs two respected open-source engines, The Sleuth Kit and PhotoRec, in one scan, then sorts the results by type and names photos and videos by the date they were taken. It only reads the drive it scans, and nothing leaves the computer.

In italiano: RecovMe recupera foto, video, musica, registrazioni, riprese di telecamere e dashcam e documenti cancellati o formattati da schede SD, chiavette USB e hard disk. Usa due motori open source molto apprezzati, The Sleuth Kit e PhotoRec, in un’unica scansione, poi ordina i risultati per tipo e rinomina foto e video con la data di scatto. Legge soltanto il disco che analizza e nulla lascia il computer.

What makes it different

  • Free all the way. Many recovery apps scan for free, then charge to save the files. RecovMe has no paid version.
  • Two engines in one scan. Recently deleted files come back with their original names; everything else is found by its content, even after a format.
  • Sorted results. Files are filed by type, photos and videos are named by the date they were taken, and duplicates are removed.
  • Honest. It tells people up front when recovery isn’t possible, such as on internal SSDs and Apple silicon Macs.
  • Safe. Read-only on the drive it scans, and it refuses to save onto that drive.
