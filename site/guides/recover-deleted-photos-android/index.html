How to Recover Deleted Photos From an Android Phone

Deleted photos or videos from an Android phone? Android wipes deleted space, so the honest answer is: they come back only if a copy survives somewhere. Here’s where to look, and how to copy every surviving file back for free, without rooting the phone.

By Dhan Denvert AsuncionUpdated 30 October 2026Mac and Windows

Your chances: it depends where the photos still exist

An Android phone can’t be searched block by block like a memory card. Its storage is encrypted and the space of deleted files is wiped. So no app can bring back a photo that is gone from everywhere. But deleted photos often survive somewhere: in a trash folder, in a chat app, or as a preview. RecovMe copies all of those in one go.

Check these places first

  • Google Photos. Open the app, then Collections › Trash (or Library › Trash). Backed-up photos stay there for a while after you delete them.
  • The gallery’s own trash. On Samsung phones, open Gallery › Menu › Recycle bin. Xiaomi, OPPO, OnePlus, vivo and Huawei galleries have a Recently deleted album.
  • A memory card. If the phone has a microSD card, take it out and follow the SD card guide: a card can be searched block by block, so it usually gives back more.

Recover the photos in 6 steps

RecovMe only copies files from the phone; it changes nothing on it. It’s free to the end: no trial, no limit, nothing to pay before you can save.

  1. Turn on USB debugging on the phone

    Open Settings › About phone and tap Build number seven times. Then open Settings › Developer options (on some phones under System) and turn on USB debugging.

  2. Connect the phone

    Use a USB cable, unlock the phone and tap Allow when it asks “Allow USB debugging?”. On Windows, some phones also need their maker’s USB driver.

  3. Open RecovMe and pick the phone

    Download RecovMe free for Mac or Windows and open it. The phone appears under Phones & Tablets in the list on the left. If it doesn’t, press refresh. RecovMe shows what it can and can’t bring back before the first scan.

  4. Choose where to look and what to find

    Under Look In, Recently Deleted & Trash, Photo Thumbnails and WhatsApp, Telegram & Other Chats are on by default. Turn on Everything in the Phone’s Folders to also copy all the phone’s own folders. Under Look For, keep Photos and Videos on.

  5. Save to your computer

    Under Save Recovered Files To, pick a folder on your computer with enough free space.

  6. Start and open your photos

    Press Start Scan, then Open Recovered Files when it’s done. Files without an extension, common in chat caches, are recognized by their content and given the right one.

When you’re done, you can turn USB debugging off again in Developer options.

Questions

Can any app recover photos deleted from an Android phone’s memory?

Only if a copy still exists somewhere, such as a trash folder, a chat app or a preview. Modern Android phones encrypt their storage and wipe deleted space, so apps that promise to scan the phone’s memory block by block can’t do it without rooting the phone, and even then the data is usually gone.

Do I need to root the phone?

No. RecovMe uses USB debugging, a standard Android setting, through Google’s own adb tool, which comes with RecovMe.

Does it change anything on the phone?

No. It only copies files to your computer. Nothing is deleted, moved or installed on the phone.

Can it recover WhatsApp photos?

It copies WhatsApp media still on the phone, statuses included. Photos that were deleted from WhatsApp’s folder and from the trash can only come back from a backup, such as WhatsApp’s own Google Drive backup.

Is RecovMe really free?

Yes, 100%. There’s no trial, no size limit and nothing to unlock after the scan. It’s open source, and donations are optional.
