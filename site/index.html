Why RecovMe Is Different

It uses the same proven engines that data recovery professionals rely on, then does the tidy-up work that usually takes hours by hand.

01

Two Engines in One Scan

The Sleuth Kit brings back recently deleted files with their original names and folders. PhotoRec then searches every block of the drive for files recognized by what is inside them, so they come back even after a format.

02

Sorted for You

Results are filed by type. Photos and videos are named and sorted by the date they were taken, and keep that date on your computer. Duplicates are removed and tiny thumbnails are set aside.

03

Made for Camera Cards

CCTV cameras and dashcams fill their cards with recording files. RecovMe searches the whole card by default, then leaves out files that were never deleted, so you only get what was lost.

04

Safe for Your Drive

It only reads the drive you scan and won't save onto it. On a Mac it can unmount the drive during the scan so nothing else writes to it.

05

Honest About Your Chances

SSDs and modern Macs wipe deleted files by themselves. RecovMe tells you up front, and points out files the drive already erased, instead of selling you false hope.

06

Private and Transparent

No account, no cloud, no tracking. The Mac app is signed and checked by Apple, and the full source code is public for anyone to review.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, 100%. There is no trial, no file limit, no paid version and nothing to unlock after the scan. RecovMe is open source, so anyone can check how it works. Donations are optional and change nothing in the app.

Can it damage my drive?

RecovMe only reads the drive you scan and refuses to save recovered files onto it, because writing there could overwrite what you want back. On a Mac it can also unmount the drive during the scan, so the system can't write to it either.

Why does my Mac ask for my password?

Reading deleted space needs direct access to the drive, which macOS only allows with your password. If a scan says it can't read the drive, allow RecovMe in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Full Disk Access.

Is it safe to install?

The Mac app is signed with an Apple Developer ID and checked by Apple (notarized), so it opens without warnings. The Windows version isn't signed yet, so Windows may show a blue SmartScreen window: choose More info and then Run anyway. Windows code signing is provided free by SignPath.io, with a certificate by SignPath Foundation. The full source code is public on GitHub. See the code signing policy.

The card was formatted. Is it too late?

Usually not. A quick format only clears the card's index. Run a Deep Scan. RecovMe searches the whole drive by default, so it finds files even after a format. File names are lost after a format, but photos and videos come back named by the date they were taken.

Some recovered files won't open.

Parts of them were overwritten by newer files, or the file was stored in pieces. That is why it matters to stop using a drive straight away. Open the important files first and copy the good ones to a safe place.

Can it recover from a CCTV camera or dashcam card?

Yes, for cameras that record MP4 video, which is most home cameras and dashcams (Tapo, Ezviz, Reolink, Xiaomi, Eufy and others). Take the card out of the camera straight away, because it records over old footage, and scan it with a card reader.

Can it recover photos from my phone?

Partly. Phones can't be searched block by block like a memory card, so RecovMe copies what still exists: on Android, the trash, gallery trash, chat apps and previews, over USB debugging; on iPhone and iPad, the backups on your computer, encrypted ones too. Files fully deleted from a phone can't be brought back by any app. If an Android phone has a memory card, scan the card in a card reader.

Does it send my files anywhere?

No. Everything happens on your computer. RecovMe has no account, no cloud and no tracking.
