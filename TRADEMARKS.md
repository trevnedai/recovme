# RecovMe Name and Logo Policy

RecovMe's code is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later): you may use, study, change and share it.
The **name "RecovMe", its logo and app icon, and the recovme.app domain** are different. They
tell people which copy is the official one, made by Dhan Denvert Asuncion, signed by him, and
updated from recovme.app. The GPL does not cover them (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).

This matters for a recovery app more than most: it runs with administrator rights and reads
whole drives, so people need to know they have the genuine program.

## You may

- Share **unmodified official builds** (from recovme.app or the GitHub Releases page) with the
  name and icon as they are.
- Say that your project is "based on RecovMe" or "a fork of RecovMe", in plain text.
- Write about RecovMe, review it, link to it and show screenshots of it.
- Use the name to refer to RecovMe in articles, videos, tutorials and comparisons.

## You may not

- Call a modified version "RecovMe", or a name that could be mistaken for it (for example
  "RecovMe Pro", "RecovMe Plus" or "Recov-Me").
- Use the RecovMe logo, app icon or banner artwork in a modified version, or as the icon of
  any other program, website or store listing.
- Sell RecovMe, or a modified version, under the RecovMe name, or put the name in paid ads
  or store listings for another product.
- Suggest that your version is made, checked or endorsed by Dhan Denvert Asuncion or RecovMe.
- Use a domain, account or app name that looks like recovme.app or the official accounts.

## Modified versions

Pick your own name and icon, and say clearly that it is a modified version, for example:
"FooRescue is based on RecovMe by Dhan Denvert Asuncion. It is not the official RecovMe."

## Questions and permission

For anything not covered here, or to report a copy that pretends to be RecovMe, open an issue
at https://github.com/trevnedai/recovme/issues or use the contact on https://recovme.app.
