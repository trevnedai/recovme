Looking for Recuva for Mac? Here’s a Free Alternative

Recuva doesn’t exist for Mac: it runs only on Windows. Most “Recuva for Mac” pages then recommend a paid app with a small free limit. Here are the honest options, with each limit checked on the maker’s own site.

By Dhan Denvert AsuncionUpdated 30 October 2026Mac and Windows

Short answer

There is no Recuva for Mac: Recuva only runs on Windows. Most “Recuva for Mac” pages then recommend a paid app whose free version stops after a few hundred megabytes. RecovMe is a free, open-source alternative for Apple silicon Macs with no recovery limit.

What Recuva users will find familiar

Recuva’s appeal is its simple wizard: pick the kind of file, pick where it was, scan. RecovMe works the same way in three screens: pick the drive, choose what to look for, then open your recovered files. Like Recuva, it brings back recently deleted files with their original names when the drive still has them. Unlike Recuva, it also runs a deep search that finds files after a format, and it sorts photos and videos by the date they were taken.

One thing no Mac app can do

Every Mac with Apple silicon encrypts its own drive and wipes deleted files by itself, so no app, RecovMe included, can scan the Mac’s internal drive for deleted files. Use the Trash, Time Machine or iCloud for those. Mac recovery apps work on what you plug in: SD cards, USB sticks and external drives.

Questions

Is there an official Recuva for Mac?

No. Recuva is made for Windows only, and there is no official Mac version. Downloads labelled “Recuva for Mac” on other sites are not the official app.

Can I run Recuva on a Mac another way?

Not usefully. A Windows virtual machine can’t read the Mac’s internal drive at a low level, and on Apple silicon Macs that drive is encrypted anyway. For cards and external drives, a native Mac app is simpler.

Does RecovMe also run on Windows?

Yes, on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), with the same features.

Is RecovMe really free?

Yes, 100%. There’s no trial, no size limit and nothing to unlock after the scan. It’s open source, and donations are optional.
